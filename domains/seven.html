Domain 7: Personal Growth and Professional Development

Continue

In this domain, I learned that personal and professional development is an important part of becoming an effective teacher, especially as a pre-service teacher during my teaching internship. Throughout my experience, I gained more confidence in handling classes, delivering lessons, and managing different classroom situations with the guidance of my cooperating teacher.

I also realized the importance of learning not only from mentors but also from my co-interns. Through collaboration, sharing of ideas, and daily interactions, I learned how teamwork and good interpersonal relationships contribute to a smoother and more effective internship experience. Pakikisama and supporting one another helped us overcome challenges and grow together professionally.

I learned that teaching is a continuous process of growth that requires reflection, humility, and openness to feedback. Every experience in the classroom and every interaction with mentors, students, and co-interns helped me improve as a future educator.

As I continue my journey, I will remain committed to lifelong learning, maintaining good relationships with others, and developing both my personal character and professional skills so I can become a more effective and compassionate teacher.
